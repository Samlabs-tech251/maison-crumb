About Us

A small oven, a big love for butter.

Maison Crumb began in a 200-square-foot kitchen with one deck oven and a sourdough starter carried home from Lyon. A decade later, we still laminate every croissant by hand, still mill our own flour blend, and still believe the best moments are the small ones — a warm pastry, a shared slice, a candle on a cake.

"We bake for the moments you'll remember — not because they were grand, but because they were sweet."
